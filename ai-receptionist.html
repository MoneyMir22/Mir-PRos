Questions

Good to know.

Will callers know it's AI?

It speaks in a natural, friendly voice and holds a real conversation. If a caller asks, it tells them honestly that it's an AI assistant for your business, then gets them what they need.

What if it can't answer a question?

It never guesses. It takes the caller's name, number and question, tells them you'll follow up, and flags it at the top of your morning recap.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep your number and set it to forward to your receptionist after hours. We walk you through it during setup. If you'd rather have a separate line, we can set one up.

What counts as a call minute?

Time your receptionist spends on the phone with callers. Most after-hours calls run two to three minutes, so 200 minutes usually covers 70 to 100 calls.

Which calendars does it work with?

Google Calendar, Outlook, Calendly and Cal.com, along with many booking tools. If you use something else, ask us on your demo call.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are month to month. Cancel anytime before your next billing date and you won't be charged again.
